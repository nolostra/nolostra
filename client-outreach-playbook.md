# Freelance Client Playbook — Saiesh Dabholkar

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** "I build and ship AI-powered products and the backends behind them — from idea to production — for small teams that don't have a senior engineer to spare."

**Niche (pick this, don't be generic):** *AI features + backend for early-stage products.* Not "full-stack developer" (crowded), but "the person who adds LLM/automation features to your product and makes the backend hold up."

**Proof you already have (lead with these):**
- CHEYNI — a full streaming + submissions platform you built end to end (cheyni.io)
- LLM-powered trading system / backtesting (shows AI + data + reliability)
- Client sites: Adi Gangesha, Nandakumaran (shows you deliver for non-technical owners)
- Stack: Node/Express, Postgres/Mongo/Redis, Next.js/TS, LangChain, vector DBs, AWS/Docker

**Offers (productized, easy to say yes to):**
| Offer | Who it's for | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| AI feature sprint | Startups with a product but no AI | 2–3 weeks, fixed scope (chatbot over their docs, summarisation, classification, agent workflow) |
| Backend rescue / scale-up | Founders with a fragile MVP | Audit + fix: auth, DB, queues, deploy, CI/CD |
| MVP build | Non-technical founders | 4–8 weeks, Next.js + Node, fixed price |
| Automation for ops | SMBs drowning in manual work | LLM + API workflows (email triage, lead qualification, reporting) |

## 2. Target segments (ranked by likelihood to convert)

1. **Seed/pre-seed startups with a non-technical founder** — need MVP or AI feature fast. Find: Product Hunt launches (last 60 days), Y Combinator "Work at a Startup", Indie Hackers, X/LinkedIn "looking for a technical co-founder / CTO".
2. **Small agencies and studios without AI capability** — want to resell AI features. Find: Clutch/GoodFirms listings of 5–30 person agencies; pitch white-label dev.
3. **Funded startups hiring a backend/AI engineer** — they have the budget and pain; offer contract instead of a hire. Find: LinkedIn/Wellfound/HN "Who is hiring" postings for backend/AI roles; reach the founder or eng lead, not HR.
4. **SMB owners with manual-heavy ops** (clinics, real-estate, logistics, coaching, e-commerce). Find: Google Maps + LinkedIn in your region and US/UK/EU.
5. **Creator/personal-brand clients** (like your yoga and speaker sites) — smaller tickets, high referral rate.

**Where to look daily (20 min):**
- Hacker News "Who is hiring" / "Freelancer? Seeking freelancer?" (monthly threads)
- LinkedIn search: `"looking for" AND ("AI developer" OR "LLM" OR "backend")`, filter Posts, past week
- X/Twitter search: `"need a developer" AI`, `"building an MVP"`, `"technical cofounder"`
- Reddit: r/forhire, r/startups, r/SaaS, r/Entrepreneur (read rules first)
- Upwork/Contra/Toptal-style marketplaces for a few early reviews
- Product Hunt "newest" launches with obvious backend/AI gaps

**Qualify before pitching:** has funding or revenue, has a live product or clear spec, a named decision-maker, a problem you can describe in one sentence.

## 3. Email templates

Rules: under 100 words, one specific observation, one ask, no attachments, send from a real name with your site in the signature. Follow up twice (day 4, day 10), then stop.

### A. Cold — startup with a live product
**Subject:** Quick idea for {Product}'s {specific feature}

Hi {First name},

I came across {Product} and noticed {specific observation, e.g. support questions are answered manually / search is keyword-only}. I build AI features and backends for early-stage teams — recently shipped CHEYNI (cheyni.io), a full streaming and submissions platform.

I think a {one-line idea, e.g. docs-aware support assistant} could cut that work meaningfully, and it's a 2–3 week build.

Open to a 15-minute call this week?

Saiesh
{portfolio URL}

### B. Cold — company hiring a backend/AI engineer
**Subject:** Contract help instead of a full hire?

Hi {First name},

Saw you're hiring for {role}. If it's useful while you search (or instead of hiring), I'm available for contract work: Node/Postgres backends, LLM integrations, AWS deploys. Examples: cheyni.io and an LLM trading/backtesting system.

Happy to take one scoped piece of the roadmap as a trial. Worth a quick chat?

Saiesh

### C. Cold — non-technical founder / MVP
**Subject:** Turning {idea} into a working MVP

Hi {First name},

Saw your post about {idea}. I build MVPs end to end (Next.js + Node + Postgres) for fixed prices and timelines — e.g. I built cheyni.io from scratch.

If you share the core user flow, I'll reply with a scope, timeline and price range, no charge. Interested?

Saiesh

### D. Agency white-label
**Subject:** Overflow dev capacity for {Agency}

Hi {First name},

I'm a full-stack/AI engineer (4+ years) who works under agencies' brands. If you have clients asking for AI chatbots, automations or custom backends you can't staff, I can take them on at a fixed rate and hand over clean, documented code.

Want me to send two relevant case studies?

Saiesh

### E. Follow-up 1 (day 4)
**Subject:** Re: {original subject}

Hi {First name}, quick bump in case this got buried. One more thought: {a second, different specific idea or useful link}. Still happy to chat for 15 minutes if timing works.

### F. Follow-up 2 (day 10, break-up)
**Subject:** Re: {original subject}

Hi {First name}, I'll stop here so I don't clutter your inbox. If {problem} becomes a priority, my details are below. Good luck with {Product}.

### G. Warm reply for an inbound/post lead (LinkedIn, X, Reddit)
Hi {Name}, saw your post about {need}. I've built {closest example, link}. Rough idea for how I'd approach it: {2 sentences}. If you share timeline and budget range, I'll send a clear scope. Calendar: {link}.

## 4. Weekly routine (5 hrs/week)
- Mon: find and qualify 15 leads into the tracker
- Tue–Wed: send 10–15 personalised emails/DMs (quality over volume)
- Thu: follow-ups; reply to everything within 24h
- Fri: post one build-in-public update (LinkedIn/X) showing a project result; update portfolio case studies

Targets: 15 leads/week → ~3 replies → ~1 call → close 1 in 4–6 calls. Expect the first client in 3–6 weeks if you stay consistent.

## 5. Portfolio fixes that raise conversion
- Add 2–3 short case studies: problem → what you built → measurable result (CHEYNI, trading system).
- Put a clear "Hire me" CTA + booking link above the fold.
- Add a testimonial from Adi Gangesha / Nandakumaran if they're happy.
- Add a one-page "AI feature sprint" offer with price range.

## 6. Compliance
Only email business addresses, include who you are and a way to opt out, and don't scrape personal emails. In EU/UK, B2B cold email to company addresses is allowed but must be relevant and identifiable.
